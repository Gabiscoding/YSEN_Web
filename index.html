A bunny is used in a lot of myths and symbolism in different cultures.

In Chinese culture: One of the 12 zodiac signs, is believed to bring longevity but also believed to symbolize having traits such as softness and merciful.

In Japanese culture: Known to appear in the tale of Moon Rabbit where it symbolizes intuition.

In native american culture: A rabbit is known for being a joker, somewhat Malicious but for that same reason— witty.

Personal bias: I love bunnies! ~~ but additionally because I believe that they symbolize softness with a sturdy attitude. Behaviorally, bunnies are known to have an attitude. They are social creatures that are naturally curious yet cautious within their environment.
